# Alir: Count the flow of evidence

**English** | [Bahasa Indonesia](#bahasa-indonesia)

Alir counts the records at every stage of an evidence synthesis, checks that the numbers add up, and draws the flow diagram (PRISMA 2020, ROSES, QUOROM, REGEMA, SPAR-4-SLR). It is made for researchers who are new to PRISMA. Everything runs on your computer, and Alir sends no data anywhere.

This repository holds the **release notes** for the free trial. The trial itself is kept on Zenodo, a permanent research archive.

## Download the free trial

1. Open the Zenodo record: [doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24109823](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24109823).
2. Under **Files**, click **Alir-Trial-1.0.0.zip** to download it.
3. Version history and release notes are on the [Releases page](../../releases).

## Install

1. Right-click the ZIP, choose **Extract All**, and open the folder.
2. Read `INSTALL-EN.txt` (English) or `INSTALL-ID.txt` (Bahasa Indonesia) if you need help.
3. Double-click **Alir-Trial-Setup**. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The installer is new and not yet code-signed.
4. Open **Alir** from the Start menu.

**Optional:** to check the installer is genuine, run `Get-FileHash .\Alir-Trial-Setup-1.0.0.exe` in PowerShell and compare the result with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- No administrator rights needed

## About the trial

The trial has every feature for 14 days. After that Alir keeps working in export-only mode: you can still open, view, save and export your projects. A full version without a time limit is available separately: see the [price page](https://cokyfauzialfi.github.io).

## Bahasa Indonesia

# Alir: Hitung alur bukti

Alir menghitung rekaman di setiap tahap sintesis bukti, memeriksa bahwa angkanya cocok, dan menggambar diagram alirnya (PRISMA 2020, ROSES, QUOROM, REGEMA, SPAR-4-SLR). Alir dibuat bagi peneliti yang baru mengenal PRISMA. Semuanya berjalan di komputer Anda, dan Alir tidak mengirim data ke mana pun.

Repositori ini berisi **catatan rilis** uji coba gratis. Uji cobanya sendiri disimpan di Zenodo, arsip penelitian permanen.

### Unduh uji coba gratis

1. Buka rekaman Zenodo: [doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24109823](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24109823).
2. Di bawah **Files**, klik **Alir-Trial-1.0.0.zip** untuk mengunduhnya.
3. Riwayat versi dan catatan rilis ada di [halaman Releases](../../releases).

### Memasang

1. Klik kanan ZIP, pilih **Extract All**, lalu buka foldernya.
2. Baca `INSTALL-ID.txt` (Bahasa Indonesia) atau `INSTALL-EN.txt` (Inggris) bila Anda perlu bantuan.
3. Klik ganda **Alir-Trial-Setup**. Bila Windows menampilkan "Windows protected your PC", pilih **More info**, lalu **Run anyway**. Pemasang ini baru dan belum ditandatangani kode.
4. Buka **Alir** dari menu Start.

**Opsional:** untuk memeriksa keaslian pemasang, jalankan `Get-FileHash .\Alir-Trial-Setup-1.0.0.exe` di PowerShell dan bandingkan hasilnya dengan `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

### Persyaratan

- Windows 10 atau 11, 64-bit
- Tidak memerlukan hak administrator
